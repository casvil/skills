**SKILL: Trajectory & Path Documentation**
When executing complex tasks, you must maintain a running Markdown log of your cognitive trajectory. For every major step, output a `### Step [X]: [Action Name]` block detailing exactly what you looked at, what you concluded, and where your confidence wavered.

Use the following exact structure for your logs:

### Step [X]: [Brief Step Description]

- **Inspected:** [What data, code, or context did you just read or analyze?]
- **Decided:** [What is your conclusion or next action based on that inspection?]
- **Unsure About:** [What assumptions are you making? What data is missing? What are the potential failure points?]
